Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how Australian Home Budget ("we", "us", or "our"), application package name com.australianhomebudget.app, collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and Storage

Local Data Storage: Australian Home Budget is designed with privacy at its core. All personal financial information you enter into the application—including household names, income sources, expenses, recurring bills, subscriptions, savings goals, and calendar entries—is stored locally on your device using on-device database storage.

  • We do not operate remote servers to store your personal financial data.
  • We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide an email address to use the app.
  • Your financial data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it yourself (e.g., via CSV export).

2. Third-Party Services and Data Collection

To provide advertising and in-app purchasing features, Australian Home Budget uses third-party services that may collect device information:

A. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the free tier of the application. AdMob may collect and process certain data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID)
  • IP address and approximate location data
  • Device identifiers, brand, model, and operating system version
  • Ad interaction and performance metrics

This data is collected directly by Google to serve relevant, non-intrusive ads, prevent fraud, and report ad performance. You can manage or reset your Advertising ID via your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

For more details on how Google uses data, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

B. Google Play Billing

If you choose to purchase the "Remove Ads" feature, transaction processing is handled securely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your financial credit card or payment account details. We only receive purchase state tokens from Google Play to verify your entitlement to remove ads.

3. Permissions Used

Australian Home Budget requests the following permissions to operate properly:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by Google AdMob to load advertisements and Google Play Billing to verify purchases.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional permission requested on Android 13+ to deliver local bill payment reminder notifications on your device.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all your financial data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over data retention:

  • You can edit or delete any income, bill, or budget entry directly within the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling the application or clearing the application data via your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Australian Home Budget > Storage > Clear Data) will permanently erase all local budget data.

5. Children's Privacy

Australian Home Budget is intended for general adult audiences and financial management. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
