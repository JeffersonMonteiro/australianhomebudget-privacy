Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2024

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Australian Home Budget (package name com.australianhomebudget.app), developed and operated by Jefferson T Monteiro ("we", "us", or "our").

Government Disclaimer: Australian Home Budget is an independent personal finance application and is NOT affiliated with, authorized by, endorsed by, or representing any government entity (including Services Australia, the Australian Taxation Office, or any Australian federal, state, or local government council). Official government information can be accessed directly at servicesaustralia.gov.au or ato.gov.au.

1. Information We Collect and Local Data Storage

Local Data Storage: Australian Home Budget is designed with user privacy as a priority. All personal financial information you enter into the application—including household names, income sources, expenses, recurring bills, subscriptions, savings goals, and calendar entries—is stored locally on your device using secure on-device database storage.

  • Developer Jefferson T Monteiro does not operate remote servers to collect, transmit, or store your personal financial data.
  • We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal identity details to use the app.
  • Your financial data stays strictly on your device unless you explicitly export it yourself.

2. Third-Party Services and Data Collection

To support monetization and app functionality, Australian Home Budget utilizes standard third-party services:

A. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the free tier of the application. AdMob may collect and process device data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID)
  • IP address and approximate location data
  • Device brand, model, and operating system version
  • Ad interaction and performance metrics

For more information on how Google processes user data, please consult Google's Privacy Policy.

B. Google Play Billing

In-app purchases (e.g. "Remove Ads") are processed directly through Google Play Billing. Developer Jefferson T Monteiro does not collect or store credit card or payment details.

3. Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by Google AdMob and Google Play Billing.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Requested on Android 13+ for on-device bill reminder alerts.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can edit or delete any data within the app at any time, or clear all app data via Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Australian Home Budget > Storage > Clear Data).

5. Children's Privacy

Australian Home Budget is intended for general adult financial planning and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
